Get your archive

Ask Snapchat for a copy of your data. It takes 2 minutes.

  1. 1
    Open the Snapchat download page Sign in with your Snapchat account if needed. ↗ accounts.snapchat.com
  2. 2
    Turn on "Export your Memories" and "Export JSON Files" Pick "All time" as the date range so nothing is left out.
  3. 3
    Submit and wait for the email "Your Snapchat data is ready" arrives within minutes or hours.
  4. 4
    Download every ZIP from the email Large exports come in several parts. Keep them zipped. The links expire after 7 days.

Drop your files

Drop all the ZIP files at once, even if there are several.
